The phone app shows what is happening in Tori on your Mac and lets you act
on it while you are away from the desk. It is a window onto the Mac, not a
second Tori: the agents keep running on the Mac, and the phone talks to it
over Tailscale. Pair it first, see
[Pairing and remote access](/docs/phone/pairing/).

## Find a session

The app opens on your projects.

- The space switcher along the bottom picks a space, like the tiles on the
  Mac. Each space shows its status bubble.
- **Projects** lists the space's projects, each with its status rollup and how
  many sessions are running.
- **Topics**, in the bottom bar, lists your [Topics](/docs/workspace/topics/)
  with their members.
- The search button searches **Projects, worktrees, sessions** at once.

Tap a project for its worktrees. Each worktree card shows its branch, the
lines changed, its commits ahead and behind, its pull request and checks, and
an issue chip, the same facts the desktop sidebar shows. A worktree the
autopilot is working in is tinted violet.

## A worktree

A worktree shows its branch and standing, then its sessions in two groups:
**Now**, what is running, and the rest as history. Tap a session to open it.

**New** opens the **New session** sheet. Pick where the session runs, then
tap **Start**:

- **Here**: in this worktree.
- **New worktree**: on a new **Branch**, made **From** the ref you type, or
  the default branch when **From** is empty. The project's setup command runs
  in it first, as on the Mac.
- **From an issue**: pick one of the issues assigned to you. Tori makes a
  worktree on the issue's suggested branch, the worktree remembers the issue,
  and an empty **First message** sends the issue's title and body.
- **Review a PR**: pick one of the pull requests waiting on your review. Tori
  makes a worktree on its head, forks included.

**New worktree**, **From an issue** and **Review a PR** show only for a
worktree that belongs to a project.

The sheet also has:

- **Agent**: the agents that run as a chat, starting on the one last used in
  this worktree.
- **Account**: shown when the agent has more than one account, with each
  account's email when Tori knows it.
- **First message**: optional; the session starts and waits when it is empty.
- **Run in background**: starts the session as background work. At the
  default execution level it runs boxed, in the agent's own sandbox. See
  [Unattended sessions](/docs/agents/unattended/).

A session you start in the foreground counts as watched while the app is
open, so at the default level it is not boxed even with the Mac's lid
closed. Pick its model and mode in the chat once it opens.

## Read and steer a chat

A chat looks the way it does on the Mac: prompts, replies, tool calls, diffs.
Scroll up and earlier history loads in pages.

- **Send a message.** While a turn runs, the box reads **Steer the running
  turn**: what you send goes into that turn, as a steer. Otherwise it starts the
  next turn.
- **Switch model or mode** from the pickers above the box. A switch applies from
  the next turn.
- **More** has **Stop** (stop the running turn), **Copy session id** and **Open
  worktree**.

### Attach photos and files

The paperclip next to the pickers attaches files to your next message:

- **Photo library**: one or more photos or images from the phone.
- **Camera**: take a photo now.
- **File**: one or more files from the phone's file picker.

Each file uploads to your Mac as soon as you pick it, into
`~/.config/tori/attachments/`, the same place the Mac's composer writes to.
Its chip reads **Uploading** until the upload finishes, then shows its label
and name; the **x** removes it. Send waits until every upload has finished, and
a message can be attachments alone.

Labels are numbered by kind within the message, `[Image 1]`, `[PDF 1]`,
`[File 1]`, and Tori adds them at the end of your text, so you can write
"compare `[Image 1]` with `[Image 2]`". The agent reads each file from its path
on the Mac.

Limits:

- A photo larger than 2048 pixels on its long edge is scaled down on the phone
  and sent as a JPEG, so camera photos fit.
- The phone sends files up to 8 MB; a larger one is refused with its size.
  Images and other files are also held to the Mac's 5 MB limit.
- Up to 10 attachments per message, and the same kinds the Mac refuses (video,
  audio, archives, Office documents) are refused here.
- Claude takes images, PDFs and any other file. The other agents take files
  only, and refuse an image or a PDF when you send.

See [Attach files and images](/docs/agents/composer/#attach-files-and-images)
for the Mac's side.

## Answer what is waiting

Whatever a session is waiting on shows as a card at the bottom of its chat,
and the session's dot turns rose in the lists:

- **A permission prompt**: **Allow Bash?** with the arguments, and **Allow
  once** or **Deny**.
- **A question**: its options as buttons, or **Or type an answer**.
- **An approval** from the autopilot: the draft, **Show the draft** to read all
  of it, and **Approve** or the other answers.
- **A worker's question**: marked **A worker asks**.

Answering from the phone is the same as answering on the Mac; the card closes on
both. To allow one command for every worker asking for it, use the Inbox's
**Permissions** tab instead.

## The Inbox

The tray button in the bottom bar opens the Inbox, with the same four tabs as
on the Mac, the first one labelled **Since**. The button counts the Needs you rows you have not answered plus the
sessions waiting for review. See [The Inbox](/docs/git/inbox/).

**Since you looked** lists what happened since you last looked: finished
sessions with their lines and verdict, pull requests, usage limits and what
still waits on you. The Inbox opens on it when it has something. It counts as
seen when you leave the tab or put the app in the background, and the Mac then
starts from the same point. See
[Since you looked](/docs/git/inbox/#since-you-looked).

**Needs you** lists what needed you in the last day, grouped by project, with
answered rows dimmed. Tap a row to open its chat, where a waiting approval or
question sits above the message box; a pull request row opens its worktree.
Opening a chat on the phone, from anywhere, answers its **Finished** rows and
its branch's pull request rows, on the Mac too.

**Permissions** lists every permission prompt waiting right now, grouped by
project and then by the command asked, the same as on the Mac. **Always allow
for all** and a number answers the whole group with **Always in this
project**, **Deny** on a row denies that one prompt, and tapping a row opens
its chat. See [Permissions](/docs/git/inbox/#permissions).

**Review** lists sessions with work you have not reviewed yet, riskiest first.
Each card shows the score, the session's name, folder and agent, its last reply,
and the signals behind the score.

- **Approve** marks the session reviewed, the same as **Mark reviewed** on the
  Mac, and the row leaves both lists. While the session is still working the
  button reads **Still working** and does nothing.
- **Reply** opens the session's chat to send it a message. It shows only while
  the session is live; an ended session has no Reply.

## The autopilot

The wheel in the bottom bar opens the autopilot, with the same time of day scene
as the Cockpit:

- **Set sail** and **Drop anchor** start and stop it.
- **Chat** is its own conversation, with **Message the autopilot** to hand it
  work.
- Decision cards for anything waiting on you.
- **Crew**, the workers running now, and **Log**, what happened.

See [Autopilot](/docs/automation/autopilot/).

## Settings

The gear opens a sheet:

- **Connection**: the Mac's address, this phone's name, the status, and
  **Disconnect**, which forgets the Mac on the phone. Revoke the phone in
  Settings > Remote on the Mac too, to remove it there.
- **Appearance**: **Tori Dark** or **Tori Light**, kept on this phone.
- **Autopilot button**: whether the wheel shows in the bottom bar.

If the Mac revokes the phone, the app says **This phone was removed from Tori.
Pair it again to reconnect.**

## When the app and Tori disagree

Tori on the Mac updates itself, but the app updates only when you install a
newer APK. When the two no longer speak the same protocol, the app says which
one to update in a banner instead of failing:

- **Tori on your Mac is too old for this app: update Tori**. Update Tori on the
  Mac, from the update notice in its title bar.
- **This app is too old for Tori on your Mac: update the app**. Install the
  newer APK from the
  [releases page](https://github.com/gettori/tori/releases). A release that
  needs a newer app says so in its notes.

## What the phone cannot do

The phone can read everything, steer, stop, answer, approve, review finished
work, start a session here, in a new worktree, from an issue or as a pull
request review, in the foreground or the background, attach photos and files,
switch a chat's model and mode, and start or stop the autopilot. It cannot
pair another device; that stays on the Mac.

## Platform

The app is for Android, as an APK on the
[releases page](https://github.com/gettori/tori/releases), not through the Play
Store. See [Install](/docs/install/). An iOS app is planned.
